Product map · v1 public release

Kids' Learning Studio

A learning home screen for children aged about 4–10 that parents install from a browser link. Missions earn stars, stars earn screen time, and everything stays on the family's own tablet.

Free · MIT licence No accounts · no tracking Works offline Fire · Android · iPad

Learning first, then the fun it earns

  1. Morning

    Today's lessons

    Timetable as pictures and what goes in the school bag.

  2. After school

  3. Reward

    Stars & time

    Stars move along the star track and turn into video and game minutes.

  4. Free time

    Videos, games, radio

    Only what the parent enabled, while earned time lasts.

  5. Evening

    Stories & calls

    Bedtime stories, optional faith module, one-tap call to family.

  6. Parent

    Report

    Daily, weekly or monthly summary, sent from the parent's own mail app.

4 · From the family version to the public v1

What changes when the server goes away

The private family version runs behind a login on a small cloud server and talks to the family dashboard. The public v1 is a set of static files: nothing to log in to, nothing to run, nothing that can leak from a server.
